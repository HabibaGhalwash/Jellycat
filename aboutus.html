About Us

Bringing Comfort, One Colorful Friend at a Time

Our Mission

Bringing Comfort
Through Color

At Jellycat, we believe that emotions deserve to be seen and embraced.
Our mission is to create comforting companions that reflect how you feel,
helping you connect with your emotions in a fun, gentle way. Each stuffed animal
is color-coded to mirror a unique feeling, offering a friend who understands,
no matter what kind of day you're having.

Our Story

Jellycat began with a simple idea: everyone deserves a friend who understands.

Inspired by the power of colors to reflect emotions, we created a collection of stuffed animals designed to bring comfort and connection.
Each color matches a unique feeling—like calm blues, joyful yellows, and warm pinks—making it easy to find a companion that resonates with you.
Today, Fluffies is more than just a store; it's a place where emotions are seen, shared, and supported. We’re grateful to be part of your journey, one color at a time.

Why We Do It

Because You Deserve
To Be Seen

We started Jellycat with a simple goal: to give people of all ages a comforting companion who "gets" them.
We’re passionate about creating toys that go beyond comfort and provide genuine connection.
Whether it’s a gift for someone special or a personal pick-me-up, our animals are here to remind you that your feelings matter and that you’re never alone.
